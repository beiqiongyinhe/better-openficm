## Style distillation: multi-round continued distillation

One round of 24 chapter samples is not enough to represent the style of a long novel. This version turns reference style distillation from "one-off sampling" into "multi-round distillation that can be pushed forward repeatedly".

- Sampling now uses a continuous window. Each round reads 24 consecutive chapters (a book without chapter titles is split into roughly 1,400-character segments) instead of the previous approach of taking 24 isolated sample points spread evenly over the whole book. A continuous window preserves paragraph transitions and dialogue rhythm, so the evidence in a single round is of higher quality.
- "Continue distillation" was added. After the first round the button turns into "Continue distillation", and each click jumps randomly forward into an unread region and takes another 24 consecutive chapters. The window only moves forward and never overlaps itself, and the jump size is bounded both by "half of the remaining chapters" and by "4 windows", so it feels like a jump while still spreading evenly along the book and necessarily converging at the end. For a 1,200-chapter book, for example, the rounds run through chapters 1-24, 100-123, 180-203, 293-316, and so on, covering the whole book in about 17 rounds.
- Evidence evolves incrementally. Each round takes the previous guide as a baseline and hands it to the model together with the new evidence for this round, instead of resending the whole historical memo. The number of rounds is not limited by context length, and each round costs a fixed 5 model requests (4 batch analyses plus 1 merge).
- The panel shows "N rounds of distillation completed, covering up to chapter X/Y"; once it has covered the end of the book "Continue distillation" is disabled, and "Start over" can clear the progress and re-scan.
- The checkpoint records which window it belongs to. Previously, clicking again after an interruption could move to a different passage of text while reusing the old memo; now the recorded window is replayed exactly as it was.
- When switching books, or when re-importing changes the content digest, the old progress is invalidated automatically and scanning starts from the beginning.

The Agent tool `read_style_source_sample` still samples evenly over the whole book and its behaviour is unchanged.

## Compatibility

- Uses the same release signing certificate as 0.7.0 onwards and can be installed as a direct upgrade.
- applicationId `com.openfic.mobile`, versionCode 12, minSdk 28, arm64-v8a only.
- Existing reference style versions are unaffected; the first time "Continue distillation" is clicked the coverage progress is recalculated from chapter 1.
